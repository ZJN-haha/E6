Spirited Away

Description

Spirited Away tells the story of Chihiro, a young girl who finds herself trapped in a mysterious and magical world of spirits. To return to the human world, she must work in a bathhouse for the spirits and find her inner strength.

Main Cast
  • Chihiro: Rumi Hiragi
  • Haku: Miyu Irino
  • Yubaba: Mari Natsuki
  • Kamaji: Bunta Sugawara
Trivia
  • Only non-English language film to win Best Animated Feature Oscar.
  • Made without a script—Miyazaki drew the story as he created it.
  • Surpassed Titanic’s box office in Japan.
